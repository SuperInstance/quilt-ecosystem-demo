5. The Convoy — 4 consensus methods + geometric median

5 honest agents at 12.4-12.6m, 1 outlier at 999m. Which method do you trust?
4 methods compared
weighted_mean176.92mpulled by outlier
weighted_median12.50mrobust
trimmed_mean12.50mdrops outlier
geometric_median12.50m1D = weighted_median
The lesson
Reyes is a sailor, not a statistician. She doesn't want to compute a mean. She wants the bottom. The substrate gives her 4 methods, all of them in one call: cell.convoy_value(method=...). She picks the robust one. The other boats trust her.

8. The 5 Best Fables — the heaviest cargo

The fables are the requirements. The substrate is the implementation. Here are 5 that shaped the design.
Fable 11 — Paper and Tablet
A picture, a conversation. The paper shows the chart silently. The tablet guesses and tells you its guesses so you can check.
"A picture is honest by staying silent. A conversation is honest by speaking its uncertainty out loud."
Fable 28 — Abacus and Cell
The abacus was a row of beads. The cell is a row of witnesses.
"Honesty without the possibility of error is just silence. Real honesty requires being wrong, and keeping the record."
Fable 29 — Lever and Decay
The lever amplifies. The decay attenuates. Both are honest about the force.
"The substrate's contribution is the ledger, not the function."
Fable 30 — Cipher and Graph
A cipher is a graph with its edges hidden. A graph is a cipher with its edges shown.
"Memory is weight. History is weight. The cost of self-reference is the point."
Fable 26 — Telescope and Tensor
A telescope sees one axis clearly. A tensor sees many axes, all declared.
"Multi-axis seeing is more accountable truth, but only if the axes are declared."

9. The Math — 5 proved, 8 resolved, 7 open

The substrate is a 13-theorem object. (Paper 117: The Substrate Math; Paper 118: The Five Theorems; Papers 119+122: Math Updates.)
WitInteg
Witness forgery computationally infeasible (sha256-128 collision resistance).
DecComp
Most recent refresh dominates. confidence_after = c_fresh * exp(-λ·Δt).
DecOrd
Freshness ranking is well-defined: write order = time order (monotonic t).
JEPACnv
Vibe (damped harmonic oscillator) converges to observation distribution. Bug fix: damping constant c=0.1 was missing in v0.1.
OpComp
Any subset of the 14-tuple has a corresponding opener. 14-tuple = (addr, val, conf, t, weight, sources, history, jepa, convoy, decay, witness, vibe, gc, graph).

10. The Temperature — Paper 124

Entropy of the witness log over a sliding time window. Cold = few writes, hot = many writes.
Frozen (T = 0)
❄️
No writes in window. Dormant.
Cold (0 < T ≤ 0.5)
🥶
1-2 op types dominate.
Warm (0.5 < T ≤ 1.5)
🌤️
3-5 op types. Mixed activity.
Hot (T > 1.5)
🔥
6+ op types. High churn.
How temperature affects behavior
  • • JEPA training: Reweight loss by 1/(T + ε) so cold cells aren't ignored
  • • Opener selection: Cold cells get cheap openers (slate, chart); hot cells get deep openers (synthesize, cross-reference)
  • • Decay rate: Hot substrates decay cells faster (λ_eff = λ · (1 + α·T))
